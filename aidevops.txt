RAG, LLM, APIs, Services and Orchestration

Please do not treat RAG, LLM, APIs, services, and orchestration as checkbox activities. During the presentations, some students were not able to clearly understand what the different services are, why they are required, and how they work together.

As discussed in the given activity, think of your application as a set of logically separated services. Depending on your application, these could include:

• Application Service – handles user interaction and application logic.
• RAG/Retrieval Service – handles query processing, retrieval, vector similarity, and providing relevant context.
• LLM Service – manages communication with the selected LLM/model through Ollama or another appropriate interface.
• Data/Knowledge Service – manages documents, knowledge sources, datasets, and related data processing.
• Embedding Service – generates embeddings for documents and queries when this is separated from the retrieval component.
• API/Gateway Service – provides the interface through which different components communicate.
• Orchestration Layer/Service – coordinates these services so that one user request can move through the complete pipeline.

For example, a user question may be received by the Application Service, passed through an API, sent to the RAG/Retrieval Service, relevant information may be obtained from the Data/Knowledge Service, the resulting context may then be passed to the LLM Service, and the final response may be returned to the user. This coordination is what we mean by orchestration.
